Frequently Asked Questions

What is VA ClaimMate?

VA ClaimMate is a free, AI-assisted web app that helps U.S. veterans organize and prepare VA disability claims. It is not affiliated with the VA and does not provide legal advice. Always review your claim with a VA-accredited VSO, attorney, or claims agent before submitting.

How does the VA combined rating calculator work?

VA uses the "whole person" method — each disability is applied to the remaining non-disabled portion of your body rather than added together. A 50% and a 30% rating don't equal 80%; they equal 65%, which rounds to 70%. VA ClaimMate walks through every step of this calculation.

What are presumptive conditions?

Presumptive conditions are disabilities VA presumes were caused by military service — no nexus letter required. Examples include Agent Orange conditions for Vietnam veterans, Gulf War syndrome conditions for SW Asia veterans, and dozens of cancers for PACT Act / burn-pit veterans.

What is a secondary condition?

A secondary condition is a disability that is caused or worsened by an existing service-connected condition. For example: Diabetes Type 2 can cause peripheral neuropathy, which can cause erectile dysfunction — each is a separately ratable condition that adds to your combined rating and monthly pay.

What is a C&P exam and how should I prepare?

A Compensation & Pension (C&P) exam is VA's medical examination of your claimed conditions. What you say and don't say directly determines your rating. VA ClaimMate provides condition-specific preparation tips — what to bring, what to describe, and what exam questions to expect.

Is my health data private?

Yes. All data is protected by database-level Row-Level Security (RLS) — only your account can ever read or modify your records. Uploaded files are read for text in-memory and are never stored on the server. You can export or permanently delete all your data from the Privacy tab at any time.

Is this affiliated with the VA?

No. VA ClaimMate is an independent, educational tool. It is not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs and does not submit claims, guarantee outcomes, or provide legal advice.

Does it cost anything?

No. VA ClaimMate is completely free to use.
